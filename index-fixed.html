Roll a 6 to bring a token out. After rolling, tap a highlighted token to move.

How to play

• Roll a 6 to start a token.
• In computer mode, RED is you and the other colors are computers.
• Tap a movable token after rolling.
• Capture opponents on non-safe squares.
• Reach the finish with an exact roll. First to finish all 4 tokens wins.

Local play and computer mode work offline.

🌐 Online Multiplayer

Online play needs a Firebase Realtime Database project. This starter includes a room-code placeholder; it will not sync across phones until Firebase is configured.
